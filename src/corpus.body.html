What this says, and where it stops

What the corpus is

A stored copy of the rated standard game archives Lichess publishes, one file per month, kept in a form that can be queried quickly.

What the check asks

Whether that copy is undamaged. Take a game out of storage, rebuild it, and you should get back the exact characters Lichess published. Every game the check compared did.

Where the verdict stops

It says nothing about whether a figure in a video is true. That is a separate check, run per video against the games it actually used. It also says nothing about a month that is not named above, and where it compared a sample it speaks for the games it drew, not for the ones it did not.

What the corpus is not

Not every game ever played on Lichess, and not a live feed. It is the published monthly archives, as published, as they stood when they were fetched.
