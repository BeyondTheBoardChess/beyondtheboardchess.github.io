# Studies

Hi, and thanks for coming! I do this for fun because I love chess and I'm curious what the numbers say about it.

Each study takes one question about how people really play and answers it from the 8 billion games Lichess has made public. [The Lichess Data page](/how-the-data-is-checked.html) shows where those games come from and how I checked them.

Nobody pays me for this, and nobody will ever have to pay to read it.
