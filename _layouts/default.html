{% seo %} {%- comment -%} Three kinds of page share this layout. Every page opens on the logo, the site's name and, under the name, the channel's links. The front page is Studies: its title and welcome, every paragraph of the welcome under the title with space between them (DA-474), then one panel per study from _data/nav.yml, newest first. Each panel reads: the Under Construction note if the study isn't finished, its question, then the picture of its question if it names one (DA-469), all inside the link, so clicking the picture opens the study. Since DA-492 each study is a tile: the question on the left, its picture on the right (DA-493), the question above the picture on a phone. A study page carries `study:` (its key in _data/nav.yml) and `tab:` (findings or method) in its front matter. It opens on a row holding the link back to all studies and, across from it, a small Findings / Method switch (DA-465); then the study's question as its title, so a long question never has to fit in the sidebar and nothing sits between the title and the page. Any other page (The Lichess Data) is its title and first line, then one panel per heading. The sidebar lists Studies, every study's full question under it on one line, then The Lichess Data (DA-472). A question too long for the sidebar ends in "..."; the arrow at the end of the Studies row widens the sidebar until every question fits, and the page narrows beside it (DA-473). Past about ten studies the list scrolls inside itself, so The Lichess Data never moves down. Above them sits the Pages button that hides the menu, and the two stay on screen together while the page scrolls, so the menu can be closed or brought back from anywhere (DA-457). Panels are cut from each page's own headings, so a page written in plain markdown gets them for free.
