{% seo %} {%- comment -%} Three kinds of page share this layout. Every page opens on the logo, the site's name and, under the name, the channel's links. The front page is Studies: its title and welcome, then one panel per study from _data/nav.yml, newest first. A study page carries `study:` (its key in _data/nav.yml) and `tab:` (findings or method) in its front matter. It opens on a link back to all studies, the study's question as its title, and a Findings / Method tab row, so a long question never has to fit in the sidebar. Any other page (The Data) is its title and first line, then one panel per heading. The sidebar lists only Studies and The Data, however many studies there are. Above them sits the Pages button that hides the menu, and the two stay on screen together while the page scrolls, so the menu can be closed or brought back from anywhere (DA-457). Panels are cut from each page's own headings, so a page written in plain markdown gets them for free. Every fixed word here comes from _data/words.yml, and a page with `month_tables: true` gets the month table, each file's fingerprint in its last column, from _includes/data-tables.md after its own sections, so that Rainy's edit pencil (DA-450, .pages.yml) can change any word without touching HTML.
